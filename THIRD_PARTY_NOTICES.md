# Android dependencies

The Android APK includes TNoodle's standalone `scrambler-min2phase` 0.20.0,
licensed under GPL-3.0. License text is bundled in the APK assets as
`licenses/TNOODLE-GPL-3.0.txt`.

Corresponding TNoodle source: https://github.com/thewca/tnoodle-lib
(version 0.20.0; published source JARs at
https://repo.maven.apache.org/maven2/org/worldcubeassociation/tnoodle/).
The Android integration source and build instructions are in this repository.

Other primary dependencies:

- Capacitor (MIT): https://github.com/ionic-team/capacitor
- Capacitor Community Bluetooth LE (MIT): https://github.com/capacitor-community/bluetooth-le
- gan-web-bluetooth (MIT): https://github.com/afedotov/gan-web-bluetooth
- RxJS (Apache-2.0): https://github.com/ReactiveX/rxjs
- aes-js (MIT): https://github.com/ricmoo/aes-js
- AndroidX (Apache-2.0): https://android.googlesource.com/platform/frameworks/support/
- Cordova framework (Apache-2.0), used internally by Capacitor: https://github.com/apache/cordova-android

Additional dependency license texts are bundled in `assets/licenses/`.
